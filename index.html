What the app does

Hyspecs Tools keeps hydraulic calculators, converted worksheet calculations, PDFs, and reference workbooks together in one mobile toolkit.

Support

For help, feedback, or corrections to calculator behaviour, email hyspecs@hyspecs.co.nz.

Privacy

The app is designed as an offline reference and calculator tool. It does not require an account and does not collect personal information. See the privacy section below for details.

Data and files

Saved favourites and calculator usage preferences stay on the device. Reference files are bundled with the app for quick access.

Privacy policy

Hyspecs Tools Privacy

Hyspecs Tools is built as an offline hydraulic calculator and reference app. It does not require a login, does not include advertising, and does not collect analytics or personal information.

Information collected

The app does not collect personal information. Calculator inputs, saved favourites, and calculator usage preferences are stored locally on the device to make the app easier to use.

Reference files

PDFs, spreadsheets, and reference documents are bundled with the app. Opening those files does not send document contents or calculator values to Hyspecs.

Support email

If you contact Hyspecs by email, your message and email address are used only to respond to your support request or app feedback.

Changes

If the app's privacy behaviour changes in the future, this page will be updated before or alongside the app update.

FAQ

Do I need an internet connection?

No. The calculators and bundled references are available on the device after installation.

Are calculator results final engineering advice?

No. Results are practical estimates and should be checked against manufacturer data, safety factors, and project requirements.

How do I request a correction or new calculator?

Email support with the calculator name, expected behaviour, and any source worksheet or reference material.
